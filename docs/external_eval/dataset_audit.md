# External UAV wildfire dataset audit (pre-registration stage, 2026-11-09)

Status: **desk audit only. No external data has been downloaded, no model has been run on it.** Every fact is marked
*verified* (read from the dataset's own page by a fetch on 2026-11-09), *unverified* (secondary source or summary) or *open* (not found).
The existing Level 1-3 baseline (`docs/baseline/`, tag `baseline-L1-3-v1`) is untouched; all external outputs go under `docs/external_eval/` and `local_data/external_eval/`.

What our frozen detectors can be scored on: single class `0: smoke`, axis-aligned boxes, RGB, 640 px inference (`eval/metrics.py`). A candidate is only usable
if it offers smoke **boxes** (or masks reducible to boxes) with smoke annotation that is complete enough to count a missed or extra detection as an error.

## FLAME (Northern Arizona pile burns, Shamsoshoara et al.)

| item | FLAME 1 (doi 10.21227/qad6-r683) | FLAME 2 (doi 10.21227/swyw-6j78) |
|---|---|---|
| Labels | frame-level Fire / Non-Fire (39,375 train, 8,617 test) and pixel masks of **fire** for 2,003 frames (verified) | frame-level Fire/NoFire and Smoke/NoSmoke per RGB/IR frame pair; no boxes or masks stated (verified) |
| Smoke boxes/masks | **none** (verified) | **none**; Smoke label rule (search snippet, unverified): applied when at least half the RGB frame is filled with smoke |
| License | CC BY 4.0 per page (verified) | CC BY 4.0 per page; "requires an IEEE DataPort Subscription" (verified) |
| Size / scenes | seven raw videos, Arizona pile burns; page lists internal inconsistencies in sizes and in which video is FLIR vs DJI (verified) | 53,541 frame pairs claimed, RGB counts sum to 53,451 (page inconsistency, verified); seven video pairs of one prescribed fire |

**Decision: do not use FLAME for box-level (mAP, localisation) evaluation.** Reasons:
1. FLAME 1 has no smoke annotation. Using it as smoke-negative data would be invalid: pile-burn frames contain unlabeled smoke, so "FLAME 1 fire frame, no smoke label" is not a true negative and every detection there would be miscounted as a false positive.
2. FLAME 2's smoke label is frame-level and rule-defined (heavy smoke), not "any visible smoke". It cannot score localisation, and the label threshold differs from our box-level notion of smoke.
3. All FLAME frames come from a few burns (7 video pairs of one fire in FLAME 2): the effective number of independent scenes is single digits, so any interval would be dominated by clustering and a one-site shift.
A frame-level secondary analysis on FLAME 2 (max detection confidence per frame vs Smoke/NoSmoke, AUROC with video-clustered bootstrap) is possible but answers a different question, needs a subscription, and is **not** part of the minimal protocol. It may be added later as clearly labeled exploratory work.

## Source overlap
- vs Boreal (Hakala et al., Scientific Data, Aug 2025, CC BY 4.0, Finland, DJI Phantom 4, 2022 burns; 4,954 images, 31 clips in our audit): no FASDD source list names it, and the temporal order (FASDD preprint 2022-2023) makes inclusion unlikely, but this is **not evidence**; it must be tested by pixel comparison (gate G3).
- vs synthetic `synth_smoke_v1`: none by construction (own generator, own textures); only domain-style similarity is possible.
- FASDD vs FLAME: FASDD claims "open-access fire datasets" as a source; FLAME may be in it. Irrelevant for the minimal protocol (FLAME is not used) but means "FASDD plus FLAME" would not be independent; the two must not be pooled.

## Domain differences to report as limitations
Heterogeneous sensors and altitudes; web/social-media imagery (compression, watermarks, framing); possibly urban or non-forest scenes and fire-dominant images; different smoke density and annotation style from Boreal's controlled restoration burns. Both models are **out of domain** on FASDD_UAV, so the comparison measures relative robustness to a third, uncontrolled domain shift, not "synthetic vs real" in isolation (Model B's training data is also drone smoke from a different distribution).

## FireMan-UAV-RGBT (Univ. Oulu; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.13732947, v1.0.0-beta.2) - desk audit 2026-11-11, read by HTTP range requests (18.7 MB of the 33 GB zip plus the 34 RGB annotation files), nothing else downloaded
Evidence: record page, README.txt, CHANGELOG.txt, the zip's file listing, all 34 RGB `annotations.xml` files (local copy `local_data/external_eval/fireman/annotations/`, gitignored) and one mid-frame per video (`fireman_midframes.jpg`).
- **Access/license:** public Zenodo files, CC BY 4.0, MD5s listed (`Multiclass.zip` 33.0 GB, `Binary.zip` 6.4 GB = fire/no-fire only, no smoke). The paper (ETFA 2024, accepted) was not read.
- **Structure (`Multiclass.zip`, 103,067 entries):** 102,923 JPEG frames, 44 CVAT `annotations.xml` (one per video), 2 modality CSVs. **No YOLO .txt files**: annotations are CVAT image-export XML in `interpolation` mode (boxes drawn at keyframes and interpolated, so not hand-checked frame by frame). RGB: 34 videos (24 train / 5 val / 5 test), 69,871 frames (the changelog says 69,942); thermal: 33,052 frames, not used.
- **Labels:** `smoke_region` is a rectangle (box); other rectangles: fire_region, lake, human, building, vehicle; tags: smoke (smoke_presence yes/no, smoke_intensity), fire, weather, camera. **64,867 of 69,871 frames (92.8%) carry at least one smoke box; 32 of 34 videos have smoke**; smoke-free: test `video_001` (329 frames) and train `video_018` (1,811), plus partial (`train/video_005`: 33 smoke frames of 869).
- **Resolutions (frames):** 3840x2160 33,575; 640x512 20,090; 958x768 8,530; 1920x1080 3,233; 958x760 2,756; 958x764 1,687. Mixed cameras and crops; all 34 sampled frames are colour RGB drone images (informal look).
- **Official test split = 5 videos, 6,009 frames, 5,680 with smoke; `test/video_002` (640x512) alone is 4,111 frames (68%)**: the official test split is effectively one video. Since nobody trains on it, evaluating on all 34 videos with a video-clustered bootstrap (about 32 clusters) is the better design; decide this before running anything.
- **Box style:** median smoke-box area 0.43 of the image and 74% edge-touching (0.5% margin, pooled over boxes): coarse whole-plume boxes like FASDD, unlike Boreal (about 30% edge-touching). Some boxes cover faint haze or the whole frame (e.g. `train/video_015`, `test/video_005`). Annotation style may mimic a domain effect; mAP50 stays primary.
- **Independence:** 32 smoke videos from four burn sites (Ruokolahti, Perho, Kestilä, Taivalkoski; dates 2022-07-06, 2022-08-16, 2023-06-14, 2023-08-26; video-to-site mapping NOT STATED). **Ruokolahti is also a Boreal event name**: overlap with Boreal is unknown and untested (needs frame hashes vs Boreal and a visual check). Perho, Kestilä and Taivalkoski would be sites Model B has never seen.
- **Status:** candidate, not yet admitted. Open gates: overlap vs Boreal (and FASDD), screening of a stratified sample, label-quality check of interpolated boxes, RGB/UAV type check of the 640x512 and 958x7xx videos, license obligations (attribution).

### FireMan-UAV-RGBT: overlap, correlation and screening results (2026-11-11; laptop only, range requests, no pod)
Method: 1,962 RGB frames hashed (30 evenly spaced per video, all 34 videos, plus extra frames from 16 videos from an aborted first pass; MD5, dHash64 lanczos/bilinear/box with the same function and `next>prev` comparison as the Boreal and FASDD hashes in `local_data/external_eval/screening_v2/hashes_v2.json.gz`, 32x32 grey thumbnails). Scripts and raw outputs: `local_data/external_eval/fireman/work/` (gitignored). Zenodo throttled the download (HTTP 429), which is why the sampling is thin.
- **Boreal overlap:** 0 MD5 matches; 0 frames within 4 bits; 2 within 6; 22 within 8 (6 videos: `train/video_003, 004, 007, 010, 013, 015`). The FireMan-to-Boreal min-distance percentiles (1%: 8, 5%: 11, median 15) are almost the same as the FASDD-to-Boreal reference (7, 9, 15), so there is no sign of duplicated footage. The nearest Boreal event is not concentrated on Ruokolahti (it is the nearest for 5 of 34 videos, Heinola 8, Evo 6, Karkkila 5, the empty-frames folder 10). The 6-8-bit hits are mostly `train/video_004`, a flat hazy-sky video, so they look like low-texture collisions, **not yet pixel-verified** (Boreal images exist only on the volume).
- **FASDD overlap:** 0 MD5; 1 frame within 4 bits (`train/video_004 frame_001040` vs `neitherFireNorSmoke_UAV010505`, unverified), 21 within 6, about 100 within 8 across 12 videos. Same caveat.
- **Same-event risk (unresolved):** Ruokolahti is both a FireMan site and a Boreal event. Hashes cannot show whether two teams filmed the same burn. The Boreal paper's per-site dates could not be read (PMC and Nature block automated access); a JYX record says Boreal was funded by the Academy of Finland project "FireMan" (decision 346710), and FireMan-UAV-RGBT acknowledges Academy of Finland grants 346208 and 348008, so a shared consortium is plausible (not verified). Which FireMan videos are the Ruokolahti ones is NOT STATED.
- **Scene clusters inside FireMan:** `test/video_001` (smoke-free, official test) has 404 cross-video frame pairs within 4 bits of `train/video_004` and `train/video_006` has 220; thumbnail correlation of those pairs is 0.996-0.997 (same stationary scene), so the official video-level split is **not scene-disjoint**. Using hash <= 6 bits plus correlation >= 0.9 with at least 3 pairs, the 34 videos form **30 scene clusters**, one of them {`test/video_001`, `train/video_003`, `004`, `005`, `006`}.
- **Frame correlation:** frames 20 apart have median thumbnail correlation 0.94-1.0 in every video; lag-1 autocorrelation of the smoke-box area series is about 0.99 or higher in most videos; the effective number of independent observations per video (integrated autocorrelation of box area) has a median of 4.6 (range 2.7-180) and sums to about 416 over the 63,309 single-or-multi-box smoke frames. Treat each scene cluster, not each frame, as one observation.
- **Interpolation artifacts:** annotations were drawn at keyframes and interpolated (CVAT). 96-99% of frames (median 97.7%; lowest video 54.5%) have box coordinates that move exactly linearly between neighbours; median segment length between slope changes is 1-116 frames per video, typically 7-50. Boxes in the middle of a segment can drift from the plume.
- **Screening sample (100 frames, seed 20261109, `local_data/external_eval/fireman/screening_v1/`, 9 sheets + `screening_manifest.csv`):** 64 smoke-box frames (2 random frames from each of the 32 smoke videos) and 36 frames with no smoke box (up to 6 per video that has some, drawn by the SHA ranking rule); 30 spot-check marks (19 + 11); each frame shows its distance to the nearest keyframe. No video was excluded. **Visual review not done.** Informal look at two sheets only: boxes are loose whole-plume rectangles that include much sky; and `train/video_012 frame 2478` shows dense smoke and a fire box but **no smoke box**, so frames without a smoke box are not verified negatives.

### Same-event question: FireMan vs Boreal (2026-11-11, public metadata, no download beyond range reads)
**Boreal dates and sites (primary source: the paper's Methods text via Europe PMC full-text XML; the PMC and Nature pages themselves block automated access):** four forest-restoration controlled burns in summer 2022, listed in the same order as the dates: Evo 2022-05-24 (E25.1856 N61.2281), Heinola 2022-06-28 (E26.4425 N61.3008), Karkkila 2022-07-06 (E23.9781 N60.6422), **Ruokolahti 2022-08-15 (E28.9222 N61.3506)**; DJI Phantom 4, 4096x2160 video, altitude about 10-200 m. Subset A has 4,954 images (Evo 931, Ruokolahti 1,767, Karkkila 1,313, Heinola 943), subset B 288 clips.
**FireMan dates and sites (README):** locations Ruokolahti, Perho, Kestila, Taivalkoski; dates 2022-07-06, 2022-08-16, 2023-06-14, 2023-08-26. The README does not say which date belongs to which place.
**What we can verify:** paired RGB-thermal videos in `Binary.zip` (`dji_video_001..014`) can be matched to Multiclass RGB videos by frame correlation (0.84-1.00; exact for 640x512 videos), and the thermal frames of seven of them carry an on-screen overlay: **2023-08-26, DJI Matrice 300 RTK, N65.456-65.476 E28.414-28.444** (Taivalkoski area). That dates five Multiclass RGB videos (`test/002`, `val/002`, `train/009`, `train/010`, `train/011`; 19,459 frames) to 2023, which cannot overlap Boreal (2022 only). Evidence per video: `fireman_video_metadata.json`.
**What we cannot verify:** the other 29 videos (50,412 frames) have no on-screen date. If the README's order holds (4th place and 4th date agree with the GPS above), FireMan's Ruokolahti burn was 2022-07-06, a different day from Boreal's Ruokolahti (2022-08-15). But FireMan's 2022-07-06 equals Boreal's Karkkila date and FireMan's 2022-08-16 is one day after Boreal's Ruokolahti date, and both datasets name an Academy of Finland "FireMan" funding line, so a shared campaign cannot be excluded. **Decision: the 29 unverified videos are excluded from the primary FireMan evaluation (reported separately as a provenance-unverified secondary set) until the FireMan authors or a paper confirm per-video dates.**

### FireMan pixel-level overlap check (2026-11-11; one CPU pod for the reference side, comparison on the laptop)
All 1,962 sampled FireMan frames (32x32 grey thumbnails, computed with the same function as the reference side) were compared with **all** 4,954 Boreal and 4,181 FASDD test images (thumbnails exported from the RunPod volume by `eval/runpod/external_eval/fireman_refexport.py`; this replaced a first attempt that fetched the FireMan frames on the pod, which produced nothing because Zenodo effectively blocked the pod). Normalised correlation at 32 px:
- **vs Boreal:** maximum 0.968, 99th percentile 0.962, median best match 0.881. **vs FASDD:** maximum 0.951, 99th percentile 0.940, median 0.856. **No frame reaches 0.97, let alone the 0.99 that exact duplicates produce** (frames of the same video 20 apart score 0.94-1.0; Binary-package frames match their Multiclass copy at 0.996-1.0).
- The median best match is already 0.86-0.88 because a 32x32 landscape thumbnail correlates with some of thousands of references by chance (bright sky over dark ground). The highest pairs were looked at (`local_data/external_eval/fireman_pixcheck_v1/pairs_01..03.jpg`): they are different scenes (for example a FireMan bog with a lake against a Boreal forest-and-lake frame), i.e. **perceptual and coarse-pixel collisions, not overlaps**.
- The 114 frames flagged by the 8-bit hash rule: 5 (Boreal) and 16 (FASDD) fall in the 0.90-0.97 band, none above. Controls (24 unflagged frames) were not needed with this all-vs-all design.
- **Limits:** only 2.8% of FireMan frames were sampled (at least 30 evenly spaced per video, so a shared shot shorter than about 1/30 of a video could be missed); 32 px correlation is coarse and does not detect crops, rotations or strong aspect changes; Boreal and FASDD images were compared at their own aspect after squashing. So: **no genuine pixel-level duplicate found; same-event (same burn, different footage) overlap is a separate question, handled by the provenance tiers above.**
